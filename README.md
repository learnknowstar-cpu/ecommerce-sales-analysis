## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to analyze e-commerce sales data and generate actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on revenue performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and data quality.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that help business stakeholders make informed decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

An e-commerce company wants to understand its sales performance and identify opportunities to improve revenue and customer retention.

The management team needs answers to questions such as:

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Are there any data-quality issues affecting reporting?

## 🔍 Key Business Findings

The analysis aims to identify:

- Revenue trends and seasonal patterns.
- Customers contributing the highest share of revenue.
- Product categories driving overall sales.
- Opportunities to improve repeat purchases.
- Data-quality issues that could affect reporting accuracy.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, potential recommendations include:

1. Develop targeted campaigns for high-value customers.
2. Introduce retention strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
3. Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
4. Investigate underperforming products.
5. Improve data validation processes to ensure reliable reporting.
